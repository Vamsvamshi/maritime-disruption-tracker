Maritime & Conflict Disruption Tracker

An intelligence-grade situational awareness dashboard tracking oil, gas, nuclear, and military incidents across strategic maritime chokepoints and the broader Middle East conflict zone. Covers the 2026 US–Israel–Iran war from February 28 to present.

Built by Deloitte Center for Energy, Resources & Industrials (ER&I) · Data refreshed 3× daily via CENTCOM RSS, UKMTO advisories, and public news sources

Geographic Coverage

🛢 Persian Gulf / Strait of Hormuz
20–25% of world seaborne oil

~20% of global LNG. Pre-war: 130 tanker transits/day. Hormuz declared closed Feb 28, 2026. MOU June 17 collapsed by July 6. Attacks ongoing.

🔴 Red Sea / Bab el-Mandeb
~10% of world oil

Houthi campaign reactivated Feb 28, 2026. Saudi Arabia's workaround closed July 23 when Houthis struck 2 Saudi tankers.

⚫ Black Sea / Sea of Azov
150+ vessels attacked

Ukraine's campaign against Russia's oil shadow fleet. 105+ vessels struck in 8 days. Campaign expanded into the open Black Sea July 15.

🟦 Caspian Sea (via CPC)
~1.5 mbd (80% of Kazakhstan exports)

Disruption via the CPC pipeline's Black Sea outlet at Novorossiysk. 5+ tanker attacks in July 2026. BTC Middle Corridor activated.

Incident Categories

🚢

Maritime / Shipping

Vessel attacks, port closures, tanker strikes, transit disruptions across all chokepoints.

☢️

Nuclear Infrastructure

US/Israeli strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities: Natanz, Fordow, Isfahan, Arak, Parchin, Bushehr.

🛒️

Oil Infrastructure

Refinery, terminal, and oil hub attacks: Kharg Island, Fujairah, Abadan, Abqaiq.

💥

Military Strike

US/coalition strikes on IRGC command, missile sites, and Iran-backed military infrastructure.

🔥

Gas Infrastructure

LNG/LPG facility attacks: Ras Laffan (world's largest gas plant) and South Pars gas field.

🎯

Base / Force Attack

Iranian strikes on US/Allied bases: Tower 22, Al Asad, NSA Bahrain, Ali Al Salem, Al Udeid.

📋

Diplomatic Events

Ceasefire attempts, MOUs, naval blockade declarations, G7/IEA emergency coordination.

⚡

Militia Operations

Iran-backed proxy operations by Houthi and Iraqi militia targeting Saudi oil and US positions.

Update Methodology

Refreshed 3 times daily at 06:00, 14:00, and 22:00 UTC via GitHub Actions polling CENTCOM RSS and UKMTO. Auto-detected incidents are flagged unverified until manually reviewed. All verified incidents link to the original source report.

⚠ Disclaimer: For situational awareness and analytical purposes only. Casualty and vessel counts vary between sources and are subject to revision. Coordinates are approximate. Not a real-time operational tool.
