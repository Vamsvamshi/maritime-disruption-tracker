Note: because Kazakhstan's Caspian production is landlocked, disruption to Caspian-sourced oil doesn't happen "in" the Caspian Sea — it happens at the CPC pipeline's Black Sea outlet at Novorossiysk. That's why this table overlaps heavily with the Black Sea section above.
